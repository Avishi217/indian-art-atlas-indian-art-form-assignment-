Indian Art Atlas

Interactive Art Map

Indian Art Atlas is an interactive digital map created to explore the geographical spread and diversity of Indian art history.

The project highlights 10 important locations across India, with each location connected to a traditional art form, artistic movement, or important historical art tradition. Users can select the numbered locations on the map or from the location list to learn more about the art associated with that region.

Each location provides:

The name and geographical region of the location

The associated Indian art form or tradition

Historical background and cultural context

Key characteristics of the art style

Representative artworks or themes

A reference image related to the tradition

Locations Covered

Ajanta Caves, Maharashtra – Buddhist mural painting

Ellora Caves, Maharashtra – Rock-cut architecture and sculpture

Mithila, Bihar – Madhubani painting

Raghurajpur, Odisha – Pattachitra painting

Srikalahasti, Andhra Pradesh – Kalamkari art

Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu – Tanjore painting

Warli Region, Maharashtra – Warli painting

Mewar / Udaipur, Rajasthan – Rajasthani miniature painting

Gond Region, Central India – Gond painting

Delhi – Mughal miniature painting

Purpose of the Project

The aim of the project is to present Indian art history through a geographical perspective. Instead of studying art traditions as isolated topics, the map shows how different forms of art developed in different regions and how local history, culture, materials, communities, and traditions influenced their development.

The project therefore combines geography, history, and visual art in an interactive format that makes it easier to explore the cultural diversity of India.

Interactive Features

Numbered locations on an India map

Clickable map points for each art location

Location navigation through the side panel

Detailed information for each selected location

Artwork images and visual references

Timeline showing the historical range of the traditions

Responsive layout for different screen sizes

Technologies Used

HTML – Structure and content

CSS – Layout, styling, and responsive design

JavaScript – Interactive map points, location selection, and dynamic information display

Images and References

Artwork images are stored locally in the project to ensure reliable display on GitHub Pages. Image credits and source references are provided with the relevant location information.

Conclusion

Indian Art Atlas demonstrates how an interactive map can be used to explore the rich geographical and cultural diversity of Indian art. By connecting places with their artistic traditions, the project provides a visual way to understand the relationship between place, history, culture, and art.
